Route points

Valhalla routes through the points; the FRC restricts each point to that road class (which picks the level on stacked roads), the optional heading picks the carriageway. The route is then placed on the OSM road network and encoded.

Encoder

Mapbox routes by travel time, OpenLR assumes shortest paths. Closer LRPs leave less room for the two to differ.
